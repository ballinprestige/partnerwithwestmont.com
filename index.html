Three pieces of paper, in this order

    A real site, from a real customer.

The three papers in detail, and the terms in plain English

What you get

Work you did not have to sell, on terms you can read

  • Your price is your price

    You quote each visit, and a route where it applies. We add our margin on top for the customer. We never ask you to come down, never renegotiate, and never show your quote to anyone.

  • Your pay never waits on our customer

    Before a job card reaches you, your pay is set aside: from Westmont's own account on jobs up to $750 of your pay, and on anything larger the customer has paid by bank transfer before the card is sent. If a customer pays late or disputes, that is between Westmont and the customer; your pay does not move.

  • Paid on your photos

    Released within two business days of a complete photo packet, by ACH that lands one to two business days later. Routes: twice a month on fixed dates. Never clawed back. If the photos show a miss, you have 48 hours to fix it, and pay releases on the fixed photos.

  • Your name on the work

    Your company is named on the customer's work order; the customer's one number stays ours. After your first job, a page for your company with the photos you consent to, and the customer is asked to review your company directly.

  • No fee of any kind

    No franchise fee, no lead fee, no exclusivity, no quota. Decline any job. If a job card lands on a site you already serve, it stays yours, direct, with no fee.

  • Nothing under your minimum

    The minimum call-out you give us is recorded against your company, and no quote request reaches you below it. Raise it any time by replying.

  • A person answers

    Kyle Reynolds reads and answers every message himself. Write to partners@partnerwithwestmont.com, or ask him to call you. Each job card names the site contact for the day.

  • Straight talk about volume

    Before your first quote we tell you, one to one, how many accounts are live in your city. We never promise volume we do not have.

  • Photo proof protects you

    Every job has a written work order, so "you never came" and scope creep do not stick. Out-of-scope requests come back to us as change orders priced by you.

In return

The one thing we ask

Do not go around us on the sites we bring you, during the term and for 12 months after you last worked there.

If a customer insists on going direct, take it, and pay a finder's fee of a quarter of that site's first-year billings. We do not approach your customers either. Both ways.

The clause is on every quote request, so you have read it before you ever quote.
