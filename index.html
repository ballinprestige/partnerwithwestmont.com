What you get

  • Jobs we sold. You never quote, chase, market or invoice.
  • Your price. You give us your rate per unit, we add our margin on top, and we never squeeze your number.
  • Paid within 48 hours of a complete photo packet on prepaid jobs, and twice a month on fixed dates for recurring work. We can promise this because we are paid before the work starts.
  • No fee of any kind: no franchise fee, no lead fee, no account purchase, no deductions you did not agree to.
  • Your name, your own customers, your crew and your schedule stay yours. You can decline any job.
  • Photo proof that protects you against "you never came" and scope creep. Every job has a written work order, and out-of-scope requests come back to us as change orders priced by you.
  • A written cure period and a written reason before anything changes.
  • Optional: help improving your own Google Business Profile, which you keep owning.

What we ask

  • Commercial general liability insurance with Westmont, and later the client on each job, named as additional insured, sent by your agent. Workers' comp where it is required. In Arizona, the §23-1601 declaration and the §23-902(D) agreement.
  • Photos and the checklist in the app on every visit. Arrive in the window, and fix photo-proven misses inside the window.
  • Water recovery or drain blocking on every wash, to keep wash water out of Phoenix storm drains.
  • Cleaning-only scope, unless you hold an Arizona ROC licence and sign a separate contract for sealing or striping.
  • On site, introduce yourselves truthfully as "Westmont's local service partner".
  • Don't take the accounts we bring you direct during the term and for 12 months after. We don't approach your own customers. Both ways.

How it works

From first email to first job

  1. You email us

    Send the details listed under "Apply by email" below. We reply to set up a call.

  2. A short call

    We explain the work, the photo checklist and how you are paid, and you ask whatever you want.

  3. The paid trial

    You run one of your own commercial jobs to our checklist. We pay $75 within 48 hours of the photo packet.

  4. Paperwork

    Your agent sends the insurance certificate. You send a W-9, proof of workers' comp where required and, in Arizona, the §23-1601 and §23-902(D) forms. You show us how you recover wash water.

  5. The subcontract

    We both sign the subcontract, which an attorney reviews before anyone signs it.

  6. Jobs

    Each job comes with a written work order. You do the work, send the photos, and are paid on the dates above.

What we never do

  • Charge you to get work
  • Pay you late because we were paid late
  • Drop you without a written reason and a chance to fix it
