About Westmont Sterling, for Phoenix cleaning partners

Westmont Sterling is an Arizona company. We sell commercial pressure washing and cleaning to businesses in Phoenix under our own name. We find the work, write the contract and guarantee the result with photo proof. Local companies do the work at their own price and are paid within 48 hours of their photos on prepaid jobs.

Who we are

Westmont Sterling LLC was formed in Arizona in August 2026. It is owned by Kyle Reynolds, who runs the company remotely; there is no Phoenix office. Westmont Sterling LLC is the company that signs the subcontract with you.

We are new. We are building our partner bench before we sell anything in Phoenix, and partners never pay us anything.

What stays yours

Your company name, your own customers, your crew and your schedule. You can decline any job, and on site you are introduced truthfully as "Westmont's local service partner", never as our employees.
