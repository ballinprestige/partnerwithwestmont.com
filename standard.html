Results, never methods

We check what the photos show, not how your crew works. Your equipment, your crew, your order of work and your technique stay yours. The lists below describe the result a customer is paying for in Phoenix and every other city we work in, plus the few rules the law or the customer's property requires.

The photo packet, every job

  • Existing condition first. On a first visit, and for any new area, a wide shot of each area and close-ups of damage or stains that will not come out, so nobody blames you for them.
  • Before and after pairs. Same angle, same framing, for each area on the checklist. Pressure washing: at least four pairs per job, with at least one per surface type.
  • Compliance shots on washes. Water recovery or drain blocking in place before spraying, and the collected water afterwards.
  • Taken on site, through the photo link. No gallery uploads, no edited or reused photos.
  • No faces, no customer names, no signage.
  • Uploaded within two hours of check-out. Junk removal disposal receipts within 24 hours.

A complete packet starts your pay clock: pay is released within two business days of it. See the terms.

The photo link on your phone

  • It opens from the job card in your phone's browser. Nothing to install; an app is optional, never required.
  • Check in and check out with one tap. The link records the time and, with your permission, the phone's location.
  • Each checklist line has its own camera button. Tick the line, take the photos, move on.
  • You see the packet as the customer will see it before you send it.

Pressure washing

Storefront sidewalks and entries, dumpster pads and enclosures, drive-thru lanes, parking-lot flatwork and ground-level exteriors.

Sidewalks, entries and frontage

  • Cones or wet-floor signs out before work starts.
  • Debris and gum gone; flatwork clean edge to edge, with no stripes or wand marks.
  • Glass, door frames, signage and painted surfaces undamaged; outlets, card readers, cameras and planters protected.
  • Clean under the entry mats; no standing water or slip hazard left at the entry.

Dumpster pads and enclosures

  • Pad, curb and enclosure walls free of grease film; gate fronts and bollards clean.
  • No runoff leaving the pad; a photo of the collected water.
  • Bins back in place and the gate closed.

Drive-thru lanes

  • Done outside service hours or inside a lane closure agreed with the store.
  • Lane, curbs and the ground under the menu board and pickup window clean.
  • No water on electrical boxes or speaker posts.

Water recovery or drain blocking on every wash where the city's stormwater rule requires it, and every city we work in does. Cleaning only: no sealing or striping unless you hold the state contractor licence and sign a separate contract for it.

Window cleaning

  • Exterior storefront glass streak-free (interior where the job card says so), in after photos taken at an angle that shows streaks.
  • Frames and sills wiped; drips and overspray gone from sills, signage and floors.
  • Work only inside your insured height limit; ladders with a second person; a water-fed pole on ground-level storefronts where it does the job.

Solar-panel cleaning

Commercial rooftop and carport arrays.

  • Existing-condition photos per string or carport row before any panel is touched, with close-ups of cracked glass, scorched cells, loose wiring and nesting.
  • Glass clean edge to edge on every panel on the card: no dust film, no dirt band along the lower frame, no droppings, no water spots.
  • Manufacturer-safe: no abrasives, no high pressure on glass or seals, no detergent runoff to drains; nobody standing on modules.
  • Nothing electrical touched. A fault you see is photographed and reported to us, never fixed.
  • Roof-safety gear on every job; the roof or carport left as you found it.
  • After photos per string or row, one wide shot of the whole array, and one line saying which strings were skipped and why.

Roof cleaning (Florida only)

  • Soft-wash runoff captured or diverted, never to the storm drain.
  • No repair, coating or sealing unless you hold the state licence and sign a separate contract for it.
  • The full roof photo checklist comes with each Florida roof quote request.

Junk removal

Commercial clean-outs: offices, retail, warehouses and tenant turnovers.

  • The work order lists what goes and what stays; items that stay are tagged and confirmed with the site contact before loading.
  • Existing-condition photos of floors, walls, door frames and the loading path before the first item moves; the path left undamaged.
  • Only ticketed items leave. No hazardous waste: anything found is photographed, left in place and reported.
  • The space left broom-swept, with after photos showing bare floor.
  • A photo of the loaded truck, tarped or tied down, before it leaves; a disposal receipt from a licensed facility on every job.
  • The city's hauler permit, licence or franchise where one is required, held by you; truck and crew insured for hauling.

On every job

  • Arrive inside the window on the job card. Running more than 30 minutes late? Message us before the window closes.
  • The same crew lead on a site where you can; tell us a day ahead if it changes.
  • On site, introduce yourselves as Westmont's local service partner.
  • Report any damage or near-miss within one hour, with photos.
  • No hand-offs to another company without our written OK.

When a photo shows a miss

The customer can flag a miss from the photos. You have 48 hours from the flag to fix it, and pay releases on the fixed photos. A paid job is never clawed back.
