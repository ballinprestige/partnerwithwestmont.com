How it works

How pressure washing jobs reach you in Phoenix

Three pieces of paper, in this order, and you sign nothing until the third. Window, solar, roof (Florida) and junk removal jobs work exactly the same way.

1. The list note

List noteYou sign: nothing

Two lines from Kyle Reynolds, sent from an address at partnerwithwestmont.com: what we do, and can we keep your number for your trade in your city.

  • Reply with the ZIPs you cover.
  • Add your minimum call-out or a link to your rates if you want to save us both a quote under it.
  • Booked solid? Say so, and when to check back. That is a yes.

No call, no form, no application, no audition, nothing to sign. You can also answer it on our keep-my-number form.

The first note never asks for a document, a bank detail, a fee or a deposit. If a message using our name does, it did not come from us.

2. The quote request

Quote requestYou sign: nothing

A real site from a customer who has asked us for a price. It carries:

  • the block and ZIP (the full address comes with the job card);
  • the scope checklist, with the area measured off the satellite view;
  • the customer's photos, with signage cropped;
  • the time to reply by, three business hours from when it is sent.

You give your number for one visit and, for three or more sites, a route number. That is your price; nothing is ever sold under it.

The request goes to the two listed companies whose ZIPs match, at once and blind. The first quote we can sell gets the job card. Your quote is never shown to another company or to the customer. If neither quote can be sold, we tell both companies and move on. The one clause we ask for (below) is on the quote link, so you have read it before you ever quote.

3. The job card

Job cardYou sign: first job only

The customer has said yes and your pay is set aside before you see the address. The card carries:

  • the full address and the site contact for the day, so your crew can get in;
  • the arrival window and the scope checklist;
  • your pay: your quote, unchanged;
  • the photo link, which opens on your phone;
  • the time to say yes or no.

On jobs up to $750 of your pay, your pay is set aside from Westmont's own account. Above that, the customer prepays by bank transfer, and the money has landed before the card is sent.

On your first job only

  • Your certificate of insurance, with Westmont as certificate holder. Your agent sends it in minutes.
  • A W-9, from you or your bookkeeper.
  • The two-page agreement, which follows the terms on this page.
  • In Arizona, the independent-business declaration (A.R.S. §23-1601) and the written agreement (§23-902(D)), at the same signing.
  • An additional-insured endorsement only when a customer's contract requires it, and we tell you which customer and why.

The terms in plain English

  1. Your price. You quote each visit, and a route where it applies. We add our margin on top for the customer. We never ask you to come down, never renegotiate after the fact, and never show your quote to anyone. If your number is above what we can sell it for, we tell you and move on.
  2. Your pay is set aside before the job card. On jobs up to $750 of your pay it comes from Westmont's own account; above that, the customer's bank transfer has landed first. Your pay never waits on our customer: if a customer pays late or disputes, that is between Westmont and the customer; your pay does not move.
  3. Pay. Released within two business days of your complete photo packet, by ACH to your business account, landing one to two business days later. Routes are paid twice a month on fixed dates. We never claw back a paid job.
  4. Misses. A miss shown in the photos gives you 48 hours from the flag to fix it, and pay releases on the fixed photos. If a customer shows us a miss after you have been paid, we ask you to fix it; if you will not, we refund the customer ourselves and stop sending you work. That is the whole penalty.
  5. Your name. Your company is named on the customer's work order. The customer's one number stays ours. On site you are introduced as Westmont's local service partner, never as our employees.
  6. No fees, no strings. No franchise fee, no lead fee, no account purchase, no deductions you did not agree to. No exclusivity, no quota. Decline any job. A site you already serve stays yours, direct, with no fee.
  7. Your minimum. The minimum call-out you give us is recorded, and no quote request reaches you below it. Change it any time by replying.
  8. The one thing we ask. Do not go around us on the sites we bring you, during the term and for 12 months after you last worked there. If a customer insists on going direct, take it and pay a finder's fee of a quarter of that site's first-year billings. We do not approach your customers either.
  9. Your photos. The photos are yours to post, without customer names or signage. Your company page goes up only after your first job, with your written yes to the page and to each photo set. The customer is asked to review your company directly, never for a reward; Westmont never reviews a company it pays.
  10. Volume. Before your first quote we tell you, one to one, how many accounts are live in your city. We never promise volume we do not have.
  11. Ending. We never drop you without a written reason and a chance to fix it.

The two-page agreement you sign at your first job follows these terms. Westmont Sterling LLC signs it with you.

What we never do

  • Ask your crew to pose as our employees.
  • Charge you to get work.
  • Pay you late because a customer paid us late.
  • Claw back a paid job.
  • Promise you volume we do not have.
  • Drop you without a written reason and a chance to fix it.

Questions

Do I have to sign anything to get on the list?

No. The list note asks only whether we can keep your number. The two-page agreement is signed at your first job card, and only if you say yes to that job.

Who sees my quote?

Nobody but us. Not the customer, and not the other company that received the same request.

How much work is there in my city?

Before your first quote, we tell you one to one how many accounts are live in your city. We never promise volume we do not have.

What if I am booked solid?

Reply and say when to check back. You stay on the list, and that counts as a yes.

Who does the customer call?

Us. The customer's one number stays Westmont's, and each job card names the site contact for the day so your crew can get in.

Who will I be dealing with?

Kyle Reynolds, who owns Westmont Sterling LLC, reads and answers every message himself. More on the about page.
