Privacy

Privacy policy

This site is for local cleaning companies in Phoenix and the other cities we work in. It collects as little as a website can.

Effective 9 November 2026. Westmont Sterling LLC, an Arizona limited liability company.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It loads nothing from other companies' servers while you read it.

What you type into a form

When you press submit on the keep-my-number form or the question form, the fields you filled in are sent to our form host, Netlify, Inc., which stores them for us. That is the only time this site sends anything to another company, and it happens only when you press submit.

We use what you send only to reply to you and to send you quote requests and job cards. We never sell or rent it. We share it only where the work needs it: your company name on a customer's work order for a job you accept, and your W-9 details in the tax filings the law requires.

What the hosting company may record

Like any web host, the company that serves these pages may keep standard server logs, such as the IP address, browser type, page requested and time, to run and secure its service. We do not use those logs for advertising and do not combine them with anything else.

When you take a job

At your first job we also receive your certificate of insurance, your W-9, the signed agreement and, in Arizona, the state forms, plus the bank details we pay you to. We use these only to run the agreement, to pay you and to keep business and tax records.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your details

Email partners@partnerwithwestmont.com and ask. We delete what you ask us to delete, keeping only what the law requires, such as tax forms and payment records.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
